AI tools for Realtors & Real Estate

  1. Lofty

    Real estate CRM

    All-in-one real estate platform with a CRM, IDX websites and AI agents. An agent could let it handle early lead follow-up and focus on showings.

    Visit Lofty official site ↗

  2. Ylopo

    Lead generation

    AI-driven digital marketing for real estate that generates and nurtures buyer and seller leads, aimed at agents and teams that run ads.

    Visit Ylopo official site ↗

  3. ReimagineHome

    Virtual staging

    AI interior design and virtual staging from a room photo. Useful for showing buyers what an empty or dated room could look like (follow your MLS rules on disclosing edited photos).

    Visit ReimagineHome official site ↗

  4. Fireflies.ai

    Meeting notes

    AI note-taker for calls and meetings. Agents could use it to capture client calls and pull out action items and buyer requirements.

    Visit Fireflies.ai official site ↗

  5. Canva

    Design & marketing

    Design tool with Canva AI features. Agents use it for listing flyers, social posts and open-house graphics.

    Visit Canva official site ↗

Also useful: general AI assistants

  • ChatGPT: Write quotes, emails, job descriptions and social posts, or talk through a business problem.
  • Claude: Draft documents, summarize long files and work through spreadsheets and plans.
