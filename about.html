How we pick tools

What gets listed, how we describe it, and how the site makes money.

What we list

Only real products with a working official website. Before a tool goes on a trade page, we open its official site and confirm the product exists and offers the AI features we mention.

How we describe tools

Each description says what the company itself says the tool does, then explains how a business in that trade would use it. We don't invent prices, ratings, user numbers or testimonials. The order on a page isn't a ranking.

Updates

AI tools change quickly. Each page shows when it was last checked (currently October 2026). Always confirm current features and pricing on the official site.

Affiliate & sponsor disclosure

Field Tool AI is free to use. We may earn money from affiliate links (if you sign up through a link, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you) and from sponsors. Sponsored placements are always labeled “Sponsor”. Affiliate deals and sponsorships don't change how we describe tools.

Spot a mistake?

If a listing is out of date, email us at hello@fieldtoolai.com.
